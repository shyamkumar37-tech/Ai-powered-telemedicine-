# Release v2.0.0 — Enterprise Healthcare & Interoperability

## Summary
Major release adding HL7 FHIR R4 interoperability, PACS DICOM medical imaging metadata, Billing & Claims, Lab Diagnostics, Spring GraphQL resolvers, and Kubernetes deployment manifests.

## What's Included
- **HL7 FHIR R4 Interoperability**: Standard FHIR resources exposed at `/api/fhir/r4/*`.
- **PACS DICOM Medical Scans**: Medical imaging metadata support (`CT`, `MRI`, `XRAY`, `ULTRASOUND`) and WADO-RS web visualization URLs.
- **Spring GraphQL Resolvers**: `@QueryMapping` patient profile and vitals query resolvers exposed under `/graphql`.
- **Billing & Claims**: Patient copay invoicing and insurance claim processing.
- **Lab Diagnostics & Immunization**: LOINC-coded lab reports and CVX-coded vaccination records.
- **Kubernetes Deployment**: Declarative production manifests in `k8s/`.
